When to bring us in

Perception that works in the lab but not in the field

Teams usually bring us in when:

  • Your perception models score well on benchmarks but fail unpredictably in rain, glare or dust.
  • You rely on raw model metrics because you lack a rigorous, scalable ground-truth validation process.
  • You are about to scale your fleet and want an independent architecture review before locking in sensor choices.
  • Your team spends its time chasing sensor fusion drift instead of building new features.
Services

Ashok Dahal, PhD

Ashok has spent more than ten years taking computer vision and machine learning from research into production. He currently leads machine learning perception work at a major US manufacturer, where he develops multi-sensor 3D perception and validates perception performance against ground truth.

Before that, he spent six years at a global technology supplier as a computer vision feature owner and designated internal expert in computer vision and machine learning. He also works as a data science expert evaluating frontier AI models. His PhD research in Computer Science at the University of North Texas developed new texture features for detecting abnormalities in medical video, and he began his career writing test-data and wafer-map software for semiconductor manufacturing.

Troy Inference Solutions operates independently of his employment. Client work never draws on any employer's confidential information, proprietary systems or vendor relationships, and he does not discuss them.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you need access to our source code?
Not by default. Most audits work from your design documents, test plans, metrics and the failure cases you choose to share. If you want code reviewed, we review it under NDA in your environment, and nothing leaves it.
Will you build or fix the perception models for us?
No. We provide the audit, the recommendations and technical guidance. Your team does the implementation and keeps full ownership of everything, including code, data, designs and IP.
Do you sign NDAs?
Yes. We are happy to sign your NDA before you share anything.
How long does a fixed-scope assessment take?
Typically two to four weeks, depending on scope. The timeline, deliverables and price are agreed before we start, and the engagement ends with a written report and a prioritized list of fixes.
How do engagements fit around your schedule?
Engagements are scoped to a set number of hours per week, with asynchronous review and scheduled calls with your team.
